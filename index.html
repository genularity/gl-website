Sovereignty

EU sovereign,
wherever it runs.

Three ways to run glasslatch. In every one, your security data stays under European control, or under yours.

  • Hosted

    glasslatch cloud, fully European.

    Hosted, operated and governed entirely in the EU, on OVHcloud, by a Swedish company. Your security data stays under EU jurisdiction, end to end.

  • Self-hosted

    Your data centre, or your sovereign cloud.

    Air-gapped included. Your security data never leaves your control.

  • Bring your own AI

    Your assistant, your permissions.

    Connect your own AI assistant, including an EU-hosted model, through the built-in MCP server. It sees only what your permissions allow. glasslatch contains no AI model of its own, and no AI vendor of ours ever sees your data.

In every mode

Data-residency checks flag any resource outside the EU/EEA.

Control

Read-only by default.

glasslatch reads. It never writes unless you allow it.

  1. Connect

    Connected in minutes.

    Access is read-only by default and connects in minutes. Choose agentless, a lightweight agent that only connects outbound, or both.

  2. Fix

    Fixes arrive as pull requests.

    By default, every fix lands as a pull request in your own repositories, and your pipeline applies it.

  3. Opt in

    One-click fixes, when you grant them.

    Direct fixes are opt-in, through a separate role that you grant and can revoke.

Questions

Where your data goes, and where it doesn’t.

What does glasslatch access?

Read-only access to your cloud, plus an optional agent that only connects outbound. Kubernetes secret values never leave the cluster.

Where does my data live?

In the EU, on the hosted service. Or wherever you run it, if you self-host.

Do you use AI on my data?

No. Connect your own assistant if you want one.

Will it change my cloud?

Only if you opt in. By default, fixes arrive as pull requests.
